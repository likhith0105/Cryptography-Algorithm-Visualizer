🌐 Applications of Cryptography

Cryptography is the backbone of modern digital security. Here are key real-world applications:

🛡️ Cybersecurity

Cryptography protects data at rest (encrypted files, databases) and in transit (HTTPS, VPNs). It ensures confidentiality, integrity, and authentication across networks and systems.

📡 Secure Communication

Protocols like TLS/SSL, SSH, and WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption rely on cryptography (AES, RSA, Diffie-Hellman) to keep messages private between sender and receiver.

✍️ Digital Signatures

Used in software distribution, blockchain transactions, legal documents, and email (PGP). RSA and ECDSA signatures verify authenticity and non-repudiation of digital messages.

🏦 Banking & E-Commerce

Online transactions, credit card payments, and blockchain use hybrid cryptosystems combining symmetric (AES) and asymmetric (RSA, ECC) encryption for speed and security.
